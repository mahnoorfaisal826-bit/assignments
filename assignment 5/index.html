There are around 10,000 living species of birds that inhabit different ecosystems from the Arctic to the Antarctic. Many species undertake long distance annual migrations, and many more perform shorter irregular journeys. Birds are found in forests, deserts, mountains, wetlands, and even cities. They build nests to protect their eggs and care for their young until they are able to survive on their own. Their beautiful colors and songs make them one of the most fascinating creatures in the natural world.
